# Changelog

## 0.0.1 (Beta)
First public beta.
- Tools:
  - Knives (flint to netherite).
  - Wooden spoon (it bonks).
  - Wooden spatula.
- Workstations:
  - Chopping boards in all 11 woods.
  - Cooking pot, frying pan, mortar and pestle, mixing bowl.
  - Stoves and brick ovens in 5 brick types.
- Crops:
  - Tomatoes on a trellis.
  - Cabbage, onion and garlic.
  - Wild variants of all four generate in the world.
- Food:
  - Meat cuts, prepared vegetables, flour and doughs.
  - 8 soups and stews.
  - Fried foods and baked goods.
  - 24 placeable plated meals, eaten bite by bite.
- Vanilla changes:
  - Vanilla food crafting and smelting recipes are replaced by the new stations.
  - Vanilla stews stack to 4.
